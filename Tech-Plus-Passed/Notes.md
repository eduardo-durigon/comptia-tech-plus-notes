## ✅ Passed CompTIA Tech+ (After 2.5 Months of Study)

Today I officially passed the **CompTIA Tech+** exam.

This marks the end of a 2.5-month journey of consistent and focused study, and the beginning of my transition into IT.

### Why I Took Tech+

I noticed that not many people talk about Tech+, and it often gets skipped in favor of more advanced certifications like A+.
However, coming from a non-technical background, I decided to start from the ground up — and I’m very glad I did.

The amount of knowledge I gained was **immense**.

Before this, my experience with computers was limited to basic tasks like emails and browsing the internet. Tech+ gave me a solid understanding of how systems, networks, and basic IT concepts actually work.

### Study Resources

The following resources were key to passing:

* CertMaster Learn
* CertMaster Practice

Both were extremely helpful in reinforcing concepts and preparing me for the exam format.

### What This Means for Me

This certification is just the beginning.

More importantly, it gave me:

* Confidence to continue in IT
* A strong foundational understanding
* Direction for what comes next

I now feel much more prepared to start studying for the **CompTIA A+** and move toward an entry-level role in **IT Support / Help Desk**.

### My Approach Going Forward

I’m not rushing this journey.

My goal is to build a **strong foundation first**, then grow step by step.

Certifications are important — but they’re not enough on their own.

That’s why I’ll continue focusing heavily on:

* 🧪 Home labs
* 🛠️ Hands-on practice
* 📚 Documenting everything I learn

I’ll keep sharing my progress, notes, and labs here on GitHub as I move forward.
