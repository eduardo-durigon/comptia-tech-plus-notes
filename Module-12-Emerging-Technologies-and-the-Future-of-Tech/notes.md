AI is widely used in:

### ⚛️ Quantum Computing  

- Designed to solve **extremely complex problems faster than classical computers**  

#### 🔹 How It Works  
- Traditional computers use **bits (0 or 1)**  
- Quantum computers use **qubits**  
- Qubits can exist as **0, 1, or both at the same time (superposition)**  

✔ Allows multiple calculations simultaneously  

---

#### 🔹 Why It’s Powerful  
- Classical computers solve problems step-by-step  
- Quantum computers explore **many possibilities at once**  

---

#### 🔹 Use Cases  
- Cryptography  
- Medicine and chemistry  
- Large-scale data analysis  
- Complex simulations  

---

#### 🔹 Security Impact  
- Could **break current encryption methods**  
- Creates need for **post-quantum cryptography**  

---

#### 🔹 Limitations  
- Expensive and complex  
- Requires special conditions  
- Not practical for everyday use yet  

✔ Likely to **work alongside classical computers**, not replace them  

---

### 🌐 Internet of Things (IoT)  

- Network of **connected smart devices**  

Examples:  
- Smart thermostats  
- Home assistants  
- Smart appliances  
- Wearables  

- Controlled via **mobile apps (Android/iOS)**  

✔ Improves convenience but increases security risks  

---

### ⚙️ Automation  

- Machines performing tasks **without human intervention**  

- Can replace repetitive or dangerous tasks  
- Humans still needed for:  
  - Decision-making  
  - Problem-solving  

✔ Improves efficiency and accuracy  

---

### 🧠 Advanced Automation  

- Devices that can interact with the human body  
- Example: brain-computer interfaces  

✔ Potential to help people with disabilities  
✔ Future possibility: controlling machines with thoughts  

---

### 🤖 Robotics  

- Robots are **machines designed to perform specific tasks**  

✔ Most robots are **not humanoid**  
✔ Usually specialized (e.g., robotic arms, delivery robots)  

---

#### 🔹 Use Cases  

- **Manufacturing** → assembly lines, welding  
- **Warehouses** → picking and packing  
- **Mining** → dangerous environments  
- **Healthcare** → surgery assistance  
- **Security** → monitoring and surveillance  
- **Transportation** → autonomous vehicles  
- **Farming** → GPS-guided efficiency  

---

### 🧍 Soft Robots  

- Designed to interact safely with humans  
- Made from **flexible materials**  
- Can handle delicate objects  

#### Challenges:  
- Not fully ready yet  
- Difficult to adapt to real-world environments  

---

### 🕶️ Virtual, Augmented & Mixed Reality  

#### 🧠 Virtual Reality (VR)  
- Fully immersive digital environment  
- Replaces real-world surroundings  

✔ Used in gaming and training simulations  

---

#### 🌍 Augmented Reality (AR)  
- Adds digital elements to the real world  
- You still see your surroundings  

✔ Enhances reality rather than replacing it  

---

#### 🔀 Mixed Reality (MR)  
- Combines VR + AR  
- Allows interaction between real and virtual environments  

---

#### 🔹 Applications  

- Training simulations (safe environments)  
- Architecture visualization  
- Healthcare  
- Remote collaboration  

---

#### 🔹 Limitations  

- Motion sickness (VR)  
- Still developing technology  
- Not widely adopted yet  

---

### ⚛️ Storage Technologies  

#### 💾 Common Storage  

- **SSD (Solid State Drive)** → fast, most common  
- **HDD (Hard Disk Drive)** → mechanical  
- External storage:  
  - USB drives  
  - Memory cards  
  - Cloud storage  

---

#### 🔥 HAMR (Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording)  

- New HDD technology  
- Uses heat to **store data more densely**  

✔ Higher storage capacity than traditional drives  

---

#### 📦 Flash Memory  

##### 2D Flash  
- Traditional storage method  
- Used in SSDs, phones, USBs  

---

##### 3D Flash Memory  
- Stacks memory vertically  

✔ Higher capacity  
✔ Lower cost per unit  
✔ More efficient use of space
